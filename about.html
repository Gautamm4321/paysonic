Electronic Tolling

Powering Digital Tolling

Electronic toll collection has fundamentally changed the way highway payments are processed. With FASTag and NETC, toll payments have evolved from conventional cash-based transactions to automated digital payments.

The next phase of this transformation is Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF)—where vehicles can pass through tolling zones without conventional barriers or stopping at physical toll booths.

India's MLFF rollout combines technologies such as FASTag, RFID-based identification, Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR), cameras and automated transaction processing to enable barrier-free tolling.

Paysonic is positioned to support this evolution through payment and transaction infrastructure designed for the next generation of electronic tolling.

Our focus is to enable the financial transaction layer behind increasingly automated mobility environments—helping create a tolling ecosystem that is faster, more efficient, transparent and digitally connected.

Beyond the Toll Plaza

Extending FASTag Beyond Tolling

FASTag has created one of the world's most significant digital mobility payment ecosystems, extending electronic payments across India's highway network.

The opportunity now extends beyond toll collection.

FASTag can support multiple mobility use cases, including parking, vehicle-related services, EV charging and other location-based transactions. India's highway ecosystem has already been exploring these broader FASTag applications.

Paysonic's vision is to contribute to this evolution by enabling payment infrastructure that allows existing digital mobility credentials and payment rails to support broader, more convenient use cases.

Our objective is simple:

One connected mobility ecosystem. Multiple payment experiences. Seamless transactions.

Transforming Parking Payments

Parking is another critical component of the connected mobility ecosystem.

Traditional parking environments often involve physical tickets, cash handling, manual verification and fragmented payment processes. Digital parking infrastructure can transform this experience through automated vehicle identification, FASTag, UPI, ANPR and integrated payment processing.

Paysonic enables the transition towards cashless, contactless and technology-enabled parking payments, helping operators improve transaction efficiency while delivering a more convenient customer experience.

The opportunity extends across:

Airports | Malls | Commercial Real Estate | Hospitals | Hotels | Transit Hubs | Municipal Parking | Residential Communities | Enterprise Campuses

By connecting vehicle identification and payment infrastructure, smart parking can become a natural extension of the digital mobility journey.
