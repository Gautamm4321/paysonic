The People Building Paysonic

Meet the leadership team driving the vision, technology, finance and business strategy behind India's enterprise-grade mobility payments infrastructure.

Our Leadership

Domain Depth. Execution Discipline. Shared Vision.

Paysonic's leadership team brings together decades of experience across banking, payments, technology and mobility infrastructure.

Each leader carries deep domain expertise in their function — strategy, finance, technology and business — while sharing a common commitment to building secure, scalable and interoperable mobility payments infrastructure.

Together, they are responsible for translating Paysonic's vision into an enterprise-grade platform connecting banks, payment networks, mobility operators, enterprises, government organizations, OEMs and consumers.

Swapnil Jambhale
Founder & CEO

As the Founder & CEO of Paysonic Technologies, Swapnil leads the company's vision, strategic direction, product innovation, and execution, with a focus on transforming the mobility payments ecosystem in India.

Under his leadership, Paysonic is building an enterprise-grade, API-first mobility payments infrastructure that brings together banks, payment networks, mobility operators, enterprises, government organizations, OEMs, and consumers through a secure, scalable, and interoperable technology platform.

Swapnil drives Paysonic's efforts to simplify complex mobility payment journeys and create seamless, connected experiences across tolling, parking, mobility services, and emerging digital payment ecosystems.

Leadership & Strategic Mandate

  • Vision & Strategy: Define Paysonic's long-term vision, business strategy, growth priorities, and market positioning.
  • Product & Technology: Lead the evolution of Paysonic's mobility payments platform, technology architecture, product roadmap, and innovation agenda.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Establish and strengthen relationships with banks, payment networks, mobility operators, OEMs, enterprises, and government institutions.
  • Business Growth: Drive enterprise business development, strategic alliances, market expansion, and key institutional relationships.
  • Innovation: Champion innovation across digital payments, mobility technology, payment infrastructure, and connected mobility.
  • Governance & Security: Ensure a strong focus on regulatory compliance, information security, operational resilience, and industry standards, including applicable RBI, NPCI, and PCI DSS requirements.
  • Leadership & Talent: Build and mentor high-performing teams across technology, product, operations, business development, and strategic functions.
Building the Future of Mobility Payments

Swapnil's leadership is focused on establishing Paysonic as a trusted technology and payments infrastructure partner for India's evolving mobility ecosystem — enabling secure, scalable, interoperable, and frictionless mobility payments at enterprise scale.
